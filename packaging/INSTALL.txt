Sipfolio — macOS preview

Requires macOS 14 or later. The package contains Apple Silicon and Intel
executable slices. No Xcode, developer account or AI key is required for
manual bottle and recipe collections.

INSTALL
Open the disk image and drag Sipfolio.app to Applications. Eject the disk
image, then open Sipfolio from Applications. For the ZIP alternative,
extract it and move Sipfolio.app to Applications before opening.

This preview uses ad-hoc signing and has not been notarized by Apple.
macOS may ask for additional confirmation on first launch. If you trust
the official download and have verified its origin, follow Apple's
instructions for approving that individual app:
https://support.apple.com/102445

FIRST USE
Choose “新收藏” (Command-N), import your own bottle photo, check the cutout,
enter a name and save. Recipes require ingredients and real instructions.
The three shelves are bottles, recipes with photographs, and text recipes.
AI is optional; configure your own DeepSeek key in Settings (Command-,).

DATA AND UPDATES
Quit Sipfolio before replacing it with a newer version. Your collection is
stored separately in ~/Library/Application Support/Sipfolio/.
To back up or migrate, quit the app and copy that entire directory,
including photos and database companion files. AI keys are in Keychain
and are not included in this backup.
